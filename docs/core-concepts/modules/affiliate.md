# Affiliate

The **Affiliate** module is a built-in add-on that allows you to create a referral program for your clients. When clients refer new users to your hosting platform, they can earn a commission on the sales they generate.

## Features

- **Unique Referral Links**: Each client gets a unique URL to share.
- **Flexible Commissions**: Support for Percentage (%) or Fixed Amount commissions.
- **Commission Triggers**: Choose whether to award commission on the *First Order Only* or *Every Order* the referred client makes.
- **Referral Scopes**: Target *New Users Only* (guests who register via the link) or *All Users* (including existing clients).
- **Payout Management**: Clients can request payouts once they reach a minimum threshold, which administrators can review.
- **Automated Tracking**: The module automatically tracks cookies and links referrals based on your configured Cookie Lifetime.

## Configuration

1. Navigate to **Admin Area** > **Plugins** > **Modules**.
2. Find the **Affiliate** module and ensure it is **Installed** and **Enabled**.
3. Click on the module settings to configure:
   - **Commission Type & Value**: Percentage (%) or Fixed Amount.
   - **Commission Trigger**: First Order Only or Every Order.
   - **Referral Scope**: New Users Only or All Users.
   - **Cookie Lifetime**: Duration (in days) the cookie stays active.
   - **Minimum Withdrawal Amount**: Balance required before an affiliate can submit a withdrawal.
   - **Auto Approve Commissions**: Choose whether to approve commissions automatically or require manual admin review.

Once enabled, clients will see a new **Affiliates** tab in their Client Area where they can copy their link and track their earnings.
