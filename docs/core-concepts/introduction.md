# Core Concepts

Welcome to the Core Concepts guide for Billmora. This section explains how Billmora structures its products, services, domains, and promotions — and how they all work together.

Understanding these concepts will help you set up your store correctly, from creating your first product to configuring advanced pricing and automation.

## Billmora's Product Hierarchy

Products in Billmora are organized in a three-level hierarchy to keep your store structured and easy for clients to navigate:

1. **Catalogs**: The top-level grouping. Use them to separate different types of services. (e.g., *Web Hosting*, *VPS*, *Software Licenses*)
2. **Packages**: The actual products or services that clients can purchase within a Catalog. (e.g., *Starter Plan*, *Pro Plan*)
3. **Variants**: Selectable options with their own pricing attached to a Package. (e.g., *Operating System*, *RAM Upgrade*)

## Plugins: Extending Billmora

In addition to products, Billmora relies on a robust Plugin architecture to automate and process the lifecycle of those products:

- **Provisionings**: Automate the creation, suspension, scaling, and termination of services (e.g. Proxmox, Pterodactyl).
- **Registrars**: Handle the registration and renewal of domain names.
- **Gateways**: Process invoice payments via third-party providers (e.g. PayPal, Duitku).
- **Modules**: General-purpose add-ons that extend the platform's features (e.g. Affiliate, Announcements).

## Why Understanding Core Concepts Matters

A solid understanding of these concepts allows you to:
- Structure your store in a way that is clear and easy to navigate for clients.
- Configure pricing and billing cycles correctly to avoid errors.
- Take full advantage of automation features like the Provisioning Plugin system.
